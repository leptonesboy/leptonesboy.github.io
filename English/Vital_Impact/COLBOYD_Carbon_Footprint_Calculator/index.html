Do you know what a carbon footprint is?

The CO₂ emissions you produce each year are one of the major variables in global warming, as they contribute to the greenhouse effect and accelerate climate change.

We can reduce our carbon footprint by turning off lights when we are not using them, recycling, riding bicycles or using public transportation, and eating less meat.
